General Information

Innovation is bottle-neck because the E2E risk mitigation process is confusing to product teams and requires extensive, duplicative data entries

The smart product profile is the source of truth for product teams bringing an idea to life. Users document common product information within the platform, which orchestrates with risk systems to identify required reviews and guide the user through the process. 
It will reduce the time required to complete risk reviews, and the funding for prolonged timelines.

The sponsor organization will be Global services. We will partner with Scale AI and Palantir to build it.
